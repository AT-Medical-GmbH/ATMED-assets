# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

AT Medical GmbH is committed to providing a professional, respectful and inclusive environment for all contributors and collaborators. All participants in this repository — whether team members or external contributors — are expected to uphold these standards.

---

## Our Standards

Behaviour that contributes positively to a constructive environment includes:

- Using professional and respectful language in all communications
- Being considerate of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully and professionally
- Focusing on the quality and integrity of contributions
- Showing courtesy and respect to all repository participants

Behaviour that is not acceptable includes:

- Offensive, derogatory or discriminatory language or comments
- Personal attacks, harassment or intimidation of any kind
- Sharing others' private or confidential information without consent
- Any conduct that would be considered unprofessional or inappropriate in a medical or clinical work environment

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions within this repository, including:

- Pull request reviews
- Issue discussions
- Commit messages and code comments
- Any other written communication in this repository

---

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the AT Medical infrastructure team at:

**Email:** [infrastructure@at-medical.com](mailto:infrastructure@at-medical.com)

All reports will be treated confidentially. The AT Medical team will review and investigate all complaints and will take appropriate action. This may include removal of comments, rejection of contributions or removal of access to the repository.

---

## Responsibilities

Repository maintainers are responsible for upholding and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with these standards.
