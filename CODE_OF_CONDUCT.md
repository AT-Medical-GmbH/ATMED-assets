# Code of Conduct — ATMED-assets

## AT Medical GmbH — Community and Collaboration Standards

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions within the **ATMED-assets** repository, including pull requests, issues, discussions and all associated communications. It applies to all contributors, collaborators, maintainers and any individual interacting with this repository in any capacity.

---

## Our Standards

AT Medical GmbH is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful and legally compliant environment for all repository interactions.

### Expected Behaviour

All participants are expected to:

- Communicate professionally and respectfully at all times
- Provide constructive and objective feedback on contributions
- Respect the intellectual property and proprietary nature of the assets contained in this repository
- Follow the contribution process defined in [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)
- Acknowledge and comply with the AT Medical Brand Guidelines
- Raise concerns or objections through appropriate channels

### Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not tolerated:

- Harassment, intimidation or discriminatory language of any kind
- Misuse, redistribution or modification of proprietary brand assets without authorisation
- Circumventing or attempting to circumvent the review and approval process
- Submission of malicious files, workflows or scripts
- Actions that could damage the reputation, intellectual property or legal standing of AT Medical GmbH
- Any conduct that violates applicable German or international law

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions within this repository. It also applies to interactions outside the repository where such conduct could reasonably be considered to affect AT Medical GmbH, its employees, contractors or the integrity of its brand assets.

---

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- Removal of the offending contribution
- Revocation of access to the repository
- Escalation to AT Medical GmbH management or legal counsel as appropriate
- Legal action where applicable under German or international law

---

## Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to:

**AT Medical GmbH**
- Email: [conduct@at-medical.com](mailto:conduct@at-medical.com)
- General enquiries: [trademark@at-medical.com](mailto:trademark@at-medical.com)
