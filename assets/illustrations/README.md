# Illustrations

This directory contains custom illustrations and decorative graphics produced for the AT Medical brand.

## Contents

Illustrations in this directory are commissioned or created specifically for AT Medical and may include:

- Hero illustrations for websites and landing pages
- Conceptual graphics for educational content and e-learning platforms
- Decorative visual elements consistent with the AT Medical visual identity
- Diagrams and infographics aligned with AT Medical brand styling

## Usage Guidelines

- Illustrations must be used in the context for which they were commissioned.
- Do not crop, recolour or alter illustrations in ways that change their meaning or appearance.
- Illustrations may not be repurposed for commercial use outside of AT Medical projects without explicit written authorisation.
- Where applicable, check individual file metadata for specific licensing or attribution requirements.
