# Brand Guidelines

This directory contains the official AT Medical Brand Guidelines and supporting brand documentation.

## Contents

The brand guidelines document the rules and principles that govern the use of all AT Medical visual identity assets. Files in this directory may include:

- The AT Medical Brand Guidelines document (PDF)
- Colour palette specifications (primary and secondary brand colours with HEX, RGB and CMYK values)
- Typography guidelines (primary and secondary typefaces, weights and usage rules)
- Logo usage rules (clear space, minimum size, prohibited uses)
- Grid and layout principles
- Tone of voice and communication guidelines
- Digital and print usage specifications

## How to Use These Guidelines

All team members, external agencies, partners and contributors working with AT Medical visual assets must read and comply with the brand guidelines before producing or publishing any branded material.

The guidelines should be consulted whenever:

- Creating new content that uses AT Medical branding
- Adapting existing branded templates for new purposes
- Briefing external designers, agencies or printers
- Reviewing third-party use of AT Medical assets
