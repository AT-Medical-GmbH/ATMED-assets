# Logos — Monochrome Variants

This directory contains official AT Medical logos in monochrome (single-colour) variants.

## About Monochrome Logos

Monochrome logos are used in contexts where colour reproduction is limited or inappropriate, such as:

- Black-and-white print materials
- Embossing, engraving or screen printing
- Situations where the background colour prevents the use of the full-colour logo
- Legal and formal documents where colour printing is not available

## Available Variants

Monochrome logos may be provided in:

- **Black** — for use on white or light backgrounds
- **White** — for use on black or dark backgrounds
- **Greyscale** — for contexts requiring tonal variation without full colour

## Usage Guidelines

- Use monochrome logos only where the full-colour or dark/light-mode variant is not suitable.
- Do not apply custom colours to monochrome logos without approval from the design team.
- Maintain the required clear space around the logo as defined in the brand guidelines.
