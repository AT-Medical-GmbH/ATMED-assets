# LinkedIn Assets

This directory contains official AT Medical brand assets formatted for use on LinkedIn.

## Contents

Assets in this directory are sized and optimised for LinkedIn platform requirements and may include:

- LinkedIn company page profile image
- LinkedIn company page cover photo / banner
- LinkedIn post graphics and announcement visuals
- LinkedIn article header images

## Usage Guidelines

- Use assets only for official AT Medical LinkedIn properties.
- Do not alter the layout, colour scheme or branding elements.
- Ensure assets are uploaded at their native resolution to maintain quality.
- Check current LinkedIn dimension requirements before uploading, as platform specifications may change over time.
