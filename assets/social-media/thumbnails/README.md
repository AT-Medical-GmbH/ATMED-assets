# Thumbnails

This directory contains branded thumbnail images for use on video and content platforms.

## Contents

Thumbnails in this directory are designed for use on video hosting and content platforms and may include:

- YouTube video thumbnails
- Webinar and online event thumbnails
- E-learning module cover images
- Podcast episode artwork

## Usage Guidelines

- Use thumbnails only for official AT Medical content.
- Do not alter the composition, text or branding elements.
- Ensure thumbnails are uploaded at the native resolution to maintain quality and sharpness.
- Thumbnails must be consistent with the AT Medical visual identity guidelines.
