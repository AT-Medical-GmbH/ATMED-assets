# Presentations

This directory contains branded slide deck templates and presentation assets for use in AT Medical internal and external presentations.

## Contents

Assets in this directory may include:

- PowerPoint (.pptx) and Keynote (.key) slide templates
- Branded presentation backgrounds and slide layouts
- Title slide and section divider templates
- Charts, graphs and data visualisation assets in the AT Medical visual style
- Icon and illustration packs approved for use within presentations

## Usage Guidelines

- Always use the provided templates as the starting point for new presentations.
- Do not alter the master slide layouts, colour schemes or typography settings defined in the templates.
- Presentations using these templates must maintain the AT Medical visual identity throughout all slides.
- Internal content added to templates is the responsibility of the author; however, the visual structure of the branded template must not be compromised.
- Export presentations to PDF before distributing externally to preserve layout integrity.
