# Documents

This directory contains branded document templates and corporate stationery for official AT Medical communications.

## Contents

Assets in this directory may include:

- Letterhead templates (Word/DOCX and PDF)
- Official report and whitepaper templates
- Contract and agreement cover page templates
- Branded invoice and quotation templates
- Internal memo and briefing templates
- Certificate and credential templates for educational programmes

## Usage Guidelines

- Always use the provided templates for official AT Medical communications.
- Do not alter the header, footer, logo placement or typographic styles defined within the templates.
- Branded document templates must not be used for personal or non-AT Medical purposes.
- When completing templates, ensure all placeholder text (indicated by square brackets or highlighted fields) is replaced with accurate information before distribution.
- Save completed documents in PDF format before sharing externally.
